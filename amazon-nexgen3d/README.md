## 2. Amazon.in rules that shape the shortlist

- **Fees:** since 16 Mar 2026, Amazon charges **zero referral fee on products priced up to ₹1,000** in most categories, including home and home décor. Pricing at or below ₹999 saves the referral fee.
- **Lamps with LEDs:** LED lamps and luminaires need **BIS CRS registration** in India. Sell lamps either with a BIS-registered bulb/cord kit bought from a certified supplier, or as a "lamp shade only".
- **No fan art or brands.** Pokémon, Marvel, Harry Potter, Nike and the like get listings removed and can lead to legal action. These were excluded even where the file was free.
- **License = right to sell.** On Snapmaker Space, **CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-ND and CC0** allow selling prints. **CC BY-NC (all NC variants)**, the **Standard Digital File License** and the **Snapmaker-Only License** don't, unless you buy a commercial license from the designer.
- **Tax:** decorative plastic articles are usually HSN **3926 40** at 18% GST, and office/desk items HSN **3926 10**. Confirm both with your CA before listing.

## 4. Recommended launch order
1. **Week 1 (no license cost):** A1 Puffer Jacket Pencil Holder, A2 Coasters, A3 Corgi Key Holder. They're fast prints, priced under ₹1,000 (zero referral fee), and good for gifting.
2. **Week 2:** buy HpInvent's and h3li0's commercial licenses. Then list B1 Interweave, B2 Vanta and B5 Crumbled Paper Pot, the vase/planter mix that sells best on Kriya and Sheyn.
3. **Later:** A4 Stump Planter and B6 Shizu as premium pieces. Add B7 Drift Lamp only once a BIS-registered light kit is sourced.

Print one sample of each first, then photograph it, measure it and weigh it for the listing.
